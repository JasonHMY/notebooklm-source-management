# Platform Playbooks

## X / Twitter

Primary use:

- Short product updates.
- Build-in-public notes.
- Changelog highlights.
- GIFs or short videos showing a single workflow.

Post patterns:

- Problem -> concrete feature -> screenshot.
- Before/after source organization.
- Release note thread with 2-4 focused points.
- Privacy and reliability notes when relevant.

Avoid:

- Overclaiming AI features that the extension does not provide.
- Posting private NotebookLM source names or notebook content.
- High-frequency automated replies or follows.

## Xiaohongshu

Primary use:

- Chinese tutorial notes.
- Screenshot carousels.
- Practical productivity workflows.
- Search-friendly titles around NotebookLM, research, source management, tags, and folders.

Post patterns:

- Cover: one clear workflow outcome.
- Body: scenario, steps, result, limitations.
- Screenshots: blur private source titles and URLs.
- Tags: keep them relevant and restrained.

Avoid:

- Direct translation of X posts.
- Claims that sound like official NotebookLM or Google support.
- Automation that logs in or posts without manual review.

## Reddit / Communities

Primary use:

- Validation and feedback.
- Supportive answers where the plugin genuinely solves the problem.
- Launch announcement only where self-promotion is allowed.

Post patterns:

- Explain the problem first.
- Disclose that this is your extension.
- Ask for feedback on specific workflows.

Avoid:

- Repeated cross-posting.
- Posting in communities with strict no-promotion rules.
- Hiding affiliation.
